## Technique/enhancement reference (EXTERNAL INPUT — unratified-until-a-run, per standing rule)

Collected reference list (outside source, not lived practice; adopt per-drink, tag results):
saline solution (2–4 drops of 20% — bitterness suppression / sweetness clarity; flagged highest-probability) · expressed citrus peel (grapefruit ↔ floral light roasts; oil not juice) · cocktail bitters at 1–2 DROPS not dashes · simple syrup 2–5 g (demerara = body) · flavored syrups ~2 g (elderflower/ginger/honey/vanilla/rosemary) · shrubs 2–4 g (vinegar-based fruit acidity) · orange-blossom/rose water at ONE drop (floral Ethiopians/Geshas; disastrous overdone) · clapped fresh herbs as aromatic garnish (mint/basil/shiso/rosemary) · clear ice (dilution + carbonation management) · atomizer mist (citrus tincture/absinthe/hydrosol) · strainer chill-then-strain (dilution control) · bar-spoon layering.

Note the shiso convergence: the external list's shiso-herb suggestion and the owned shiso bitters point at the same flavor direction from two sources.

## Charter (what this lane IS and ISN'T)

- **Low rigor by design.** No protocol docs, no Coordinator/Assistant machinery, no hypothesis pre-statement, no TDS unless curiosity demands. Make the drink, enjoy it, jot what's worth jotting.
- **Not competition work** — but it's deliberately adjacent: serving-side technique (saline, bitters-dosing discipline, clarification, ice/dilution craft) is the kind of cross-discipline material that could inform a future routine's presentation layer, and RP8's own history shows off-book play seeding real projects (the Barbie Beans session WAS this lane before it had a name).
- **Graduation path:** a drink that produces a durable, repeatable finding worth building on → gets a § Side quests line of its own or scopes into a research project via the normal talk-through. A finding that touches canon (vocabulary, a serving-form question, a modifier candidate) → routes to the grilling queue like anything else.
- **No push/archival obligations.** If a recipe becomes a keeper the operator wants durably recorded, that's a deliberate one-off decision at that point (and intersects the item-56 representation outcome for concentrated bases).
