## What the project asked, and what it answered

**The bet (operator, 2026-07-29):** a radically different self-invented brewing method as a WBC routine-differentiation element — can a conventional paper-filtered brewer produce espresso-adjacent concentration with filter clarity, served undiluted, as a style with its own recipe logic?

**The answer: yes, and it is now a mapped style, not a hunch.** Five tracks, five coffees (El Oasis delicate washed Gesha · Motta extraction-demanding anaerobic washed Gesha · Loud Giants Pacamara natural · Chombi NASD Gesha · Project One Peach yeast-anaerobic Catimor), four mechanisms, ~22 scored cups, every trial doc-archived, zero vials wasted to protocol failure.

## The mechanism map (T5 Finding 7 — the close-out-shaping statement, carried as drafted)

With all four mechanisms read — **grind (T1/T2)** sets the gravity ladder and its 1:9 boundary and carries the tannin tax; **valve (T3)** is percolation's best form (contact control without grind pressure); **drops (T4)** is a serving-side layer on the finished cup (locked office dose, blind-verified); **press (T5)** is the boundary-breaker and the astringency-free form, reaching below 1:9 coherently and reproducing on profile — the style's best forms are now two: **valve-driven percolation at 1:10** (percolation clarity character) and **immersion-press at 1:10-strength** (round, layered, astringency-free; arrived at directly at 1:10 or via 1:9 + ~20% bypass), with drops available on either as serving-side enhancement. **Mechanism choice is a character choice, not a quality ranking; the boundary and the failure modes, not the optimum strength, are what change with mechanism.**

## Headline findings (cross-track, consolidated)

1. **The style is real, reproducible, and its own thing.** 1:10 beat or matched each coffee's optimized normal-ratio control as a *distinct liked style* (flavor transformation, not intensification — T1's honey→caramel/wine-grape; T2's punchier candied front). Reproduced on three mechanisms (T2 percolation Δ0.01% · T3 valve marginal · T5 press profile-reproduced).
2. **Optimum strength is mechanism-independent (~1:10-strength); the BOUNDARY is mechanism-dependent.** Percolation collapses at 1:9→1:8 on every coffee tested; immersion-press is coherent at 1:7.5. Boundary statements must name the mechanism (item 56 vocabulary input).
3. **Failure signatures are coffee-structural, not style constants:** El Oasis hollow-mid+tannin collapse · Motta split-balance incoherence · valve over-restriction mono-front-with-LOWER-TDS (restriction is not monotone with extraction) · press showed no astringency failure at all on the pressure-punishing coffee.
4. **The tannin tax is grind-driven** (T3 constant-grind valve test; T5 press confirmation at 7.0). Grind-compensation buys strength at a texture cost the other contact mechanisms don't pay. Grind coefficients (−0.07 to −0.1/pt) are taste-fit starting rules only — never iso-extraction claims (EY slid or held depending on coffee).
5. **The no-bypass identity amends to "no dilution past optimum."** Bypass-to-optimum from a stronger concentrate WON blind against the operator's own known-identity prior (T5 buffer — the project's strongest-shaped single piece of evidence); bypass-past-optimum lost. The champion concentrate+bypass architecture is right about bypass as strength-targeting; RP8 keeps the undiluted *default* and sanctions bypass-to-optimum as a serving form. **Primary item-56 feed.**
6. **Finished-cup drops survive concentration:** the locked office 1 TONIK + 1 JAMM won T4's blind tournament at ~4× practiced per-mL intensity (not ratio-conditional); Konflux inverts to texture-completion at concentration but lost the final. Serving-side layer, either mechanism.
7. **Stage behavior is coffee-dependent, not style-intrinsic** (T1 compressed; T2 kept its cool peak; T5's cooling-dependent coffee never read disjointed hot). The staged hot/warm/cool read stays as instrumentation; no style-level temperature claim is warranted.
8. **Prior-art position sharpened:** WBC corpus has one concentrated routine (Paprik Liu 2026, hardware/water-driven); WAC champions all bypass. The operator's build — conventional dripper or capless AeroPress, grind-gentle, locked minimal water, undiluted-default, warm-served dessert framing — occupies a lane nobody in either corpus holds.
