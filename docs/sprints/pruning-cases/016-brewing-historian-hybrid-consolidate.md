Neither large doc was over its 80 KB per-doc cap; the diagnosis was growth-vector: hybrid.md had grown +12 KB since July at 1.5-4.7 KB per Hybrid confirmation (4-5 per quarter), restating the same five rules in every entry (late-cut rule 7×, open-phase-is-a-rinse 5×, Temperature-Staged-via-ICS 4×, temperature-is-the-reach-lever 3×, April-vs-cone routing 3×). - **Shape(s) used:** **`consolidate`** (primary - the case-007 shape applied one level down: a strategy capsule had itself become a chronological rollup; the rules are now the body and the per-brew detail is a ledger row pointing at the DB brew row, with Latent lots also carrying a roasting-historian learnings capsule); **`re-home`** (Pink Bourbon n≥3 → its own capsule under the uniform n-threshold rule; byte-neutral inside the cluster, done for rule compliance not size); **`delete`** limited to the empty `### Roast Level` heading.
- **Judgment calls:** (1) **Rules-once + ledger rather than strip-restatements-only** - the light alternative (-12 KB) would have re-tripped in 2-3 quarters; the consolidate changes the file's growth rate because a new confirmation now adds a ledger row (~0.3 KB) plus any genuinely new rule, not a 2-4 KB narrative. (2) **Ledger sidesteps the source's inconsistent ordinal numbering** (Mount Elgon and Pacamara were both "THIRD" ICS; Bukure and NO°5 both "FOURTH") by sorting on date. (3) **Three pre-framework Sequential reclassifications have no brew_id** - kept as ledger rows with `-`, not dropped. (4) **The Khun Lao Switch tail-cut note was kept as prose** (not just a ledger lesson) because `brewing-equipment-expert/cluster/operational-reference.md`'s Hario Switch row names hybrid.md as its canonical home. (5) **CCIL Pattern #2's two confirmation paragraphs became rule bullets, not deletes** - the "temperature leads in BOTH directions" and the updated Suppression-fails fallback ladder were rule content that had never been lifted out of the narrative. (6) **Rosado stays sub-threshold** (n=1, a sibling variety) with a pointer to the new Pink Bourbon capsule, rather than being folded in as a Pink Bourbon data point. (7) **The brief's hypothesis** (RP9 Track 2/3 appends inflating the cluster) was tested: the two arbitration appends were 2 lines each; the mass was pre-existing narrative accumulation.
- **Heuristic learned:** **A by-axis capsule accretes the same way the CCIL did before case 007 - chronological "Nth CONFIRMED" entries that each restate the rules - and the fix is the same shape one level down: rules-once as the body, a ledger with IDs as the audit trail, detail in the system of record.** The tell is a rule stated ≥3 times across entries. Second: **the ledger-row discipline should be the append convention going forward**, not a prune-time rewrite - `propose_doc_changes` for a new Hybrid confirmation should target the ledger + any new rule bullet. (Flagged for the next `/brew` + arbiter-rule touch; not changed in this case.)

## Shape-coverage note

Deliberately `consolidate`, not `split`.
