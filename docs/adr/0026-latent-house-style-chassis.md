# Latent house style is a Hybrid (Sequential) chassis; intensity strategies are named deltas on it

Ratified 2026-10-06 (house-style grill, grilling-queue item 59). Forty brews (May-Sept 2026) had converged on one SWORKS recipe shape regardless of label (hold the front at Dial 5, build two layers, open at the end, own contact time with the valve), while the substrate still presented brewing as design-from-scratch per coffee. Chris named the converged shape the **Latent house style** and we chose to (1) make it the default recipe the `/brew` skill and the operational guide start from, (2) label a plain house-style brew **Hybrid (Sequential)** per the standing v8.4 rule "a valve phase boundary = Sequential Hybrid", and (3) keep the five intensity strategies as **named parameter deltas on the chassis** rather than add a `chassis` column and demote strategy to a note. The Two-Axis Framework stays the taxonomy of intent; `extraction_strategy` stays the recorded label; no schema change.

## Consequences

- Seven SWORKS brews archived as Balanced Intensity with a restricted→open valve transition were relabelled Hybrid (Sequential) with a `strategy_notes` append (La Dinastia 23b48be7 · Zarza Bella Vista e560bc72 · Mokkita Cold Room 7ad09c9b · Blue Iris f404e3b0 · Janson 1010 64606db8 · El Placer 99ce6fa9 · Alo Gemechu 265fdff8). Balanced Intensity nearly disappears from SWORKS brews; that is a true statement about practice, not a loss. It survives on constant-valve and valveless brews.
- Rejected alternative: a `chassis` field on `brews` with strategy demoted. It would have churned 40+ rows and the MCP schema for a distinction one paragraph of prose carries, and would have split "intent" from "recipe" at the record level when the Extraction Strategy entry already declares strategies intent-defined.
- Rejected alternative: house style = Balanced Intensity with the v8.4 rule narrowed to "deliberate mode switch". It would have made two labels true of one recipe, which is the drift this grill exists to close.
- The house style is also the roasting-side design target: the SPG standing recipe starts from it, the optimized brew of a self-roasted lot is it by default, and a self-roasted brew that leaves it records why in one line. Exceptions become countable; a recurring exception is the signal for a new adjustment row.
- Brewer rotation discipline inverts from "propose the strategy-fit brewer even if under-used" to "SWORKS by default, leave on four named triggers". The default-brewer trap remains an analytical headword but applies only once the default has been left.
- Full Expression has no observed chassis delta and is recorded as such; it is not to be back-filled from a third-party roaster's doctrine.
