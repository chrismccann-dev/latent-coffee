## Working hypothesis (final V1 design basis, 2026-08-11)

The development window sits on the **bean-temp drop-ceiling axis, not peak inlet** (FC temp architectural at ~204-205°C on naturals; peak inlet moves FC time only). The design rests on **two resolved same-family lots, closed 2026-08, that resolved at OPPOSITE ends of the drop ladder**:

- **Wush Wush Natural** (ref Batch 220, closed 2026-08-10): **FC-onset won** - 206°C auto-drop, 4s dev, ground 79.1, full producer-notes cup, zero roast register, body brew-recoverable. Protocol lessons: bracket ceilings in ~1°C steps (their 2°C step left the decisive ~207 middle unsampled); development is color-driven, not dev-time-driven.
- **Bukure AN10 mild anaerobic** (ref Batch 218, closed 2026-08-08): **the more-developed slot won** - 209°C auto-drop (FC+4), ferment integrated into depth, zero roast character; ceiling brackets 209-clean / 211-over. Protocol lessons: the **sustained post-FC inlet tail** reaches 207-209°C targets on genuine dev energy (no bake, no clock-cap); Agtron slope ~3 pts/°C of drop above ~207 on that tail; **underdev masks as attractive brightness at the xbloom gate** (N=2 inversions) - real-pourover arbiter mandatory between close candidates; honest auto-drop, never manual holds.

The discriminator between the two solutions appears to be **ferment intensity** - and this lot's bag-open funk puts it toward the AN10 side. V1 brackets both solutions rather than presuming either. Target register: WB ~71-81 centered ~75-78; the overdev ceiling is apex-defined (cup going flat/loud/static across the arc), not a color. On the AN10 family, widest WB→ground delta tracked the winning depth style (N=4) - do not auto-read wide delta as underdevelopment here.
