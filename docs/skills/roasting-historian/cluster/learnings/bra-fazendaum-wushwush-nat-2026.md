## The arc (V1 -> V3)

- **V1 (peak inlet sweep 245/248/251C):** all three breached structural failure boundaries; FC arrived at 204.7-205.6C regardless of peak (peak moves FC TIME, not FC TEMP). Cup hierarchy INVERTED structure (worst batch preferred) - later resolved as an all-past-FC artifact, not preference. Producer notes absent in every cup.
- **V2 (early-ramp lever at 242C peak, bean-temp drops 200/202/204C):** hypothesis "gentler ramp moves FC temp cooler" FALSIFIED - FC never fired on any batch. But the cup side validated the lighter direction: mandarin first emerged at ground ~90.7 ("idea forming") and read clearly on v2c (Batch 208, ground 87.5) with V1's dark-tea wall gone. Net claim: FC TEMP is fixed at ~205C on this lot across the tested design space.
- **V3 (single-variable ceiling ladder on v2c's curve: 206/208/210C):** first V-set with all three FC AUDIBLE (205.5-205.8C - fixed-FC 3-for-3). Came out bimodal, not a ladder: 4s / 61s / 80s dev (post-FC climb ~1C/30s made the +2C step buy +57s). **v3a (Batch 220) won**: first full producer-notes cup (mandarin + prune + cacao-as-fruit), zero roast register, ground 79.1. 221 (ground 68.5) and 222 (ground 64.6, clock-capped - ~208C is the curve's practical ceiling) both crossed the roast-character line. SPG waived (clear verdict + green exhausted).

## Reference roast - Batch 220 (v3a)

- v2c's parent curve (peak 242C, early ramp 200->232->240), bean-temp auto-drop 206C against the fixed ~205C FC. FC 4:43/205.7C, drop 4:47/206.0C, 4s dev (1.4%). WB Agtron 78.6 / ground 79.1 / delta -0.5. Weight loss 9.6%.
- Why it won: the lot resolves at FC-onset - development is color-driven, not dev-time-driven; mandarin was fully formed at 4s dev. Thin body was the only deficit and proved brew-side-recoverable. The roast-character tolerance boundary sits between ground 79.1 and 68.5 (72-76 middle unsampled; a ~207C slot is the untested fill if this green ever returns).

## Response surface (V2+V3, 6 cupped batches, ground ~94 to ~65)

Mandarin emerges ~ground 87-88, completes ~79, drowns under roast register by ~68. Underdev: grassy/vegetal/hay (no-FC zone), then bright-but-hollow. Overdev: roast character + roast-toned finish, dark-tea register returns. WB->Gnd sign inversion recurred 4x (v1b/v2b/v2c/v3a), always on the lightest batch - light-end quirk, NOT a cup-quality kill signal here (the 4th inversion sat on the reference roast).

## Optimized brew

- **brew_id:** 8cad201d-64e5-494d-9d6d-06376b665e25 (2026-08-10) · Phase-mapped Hybrid on Sworks Bottomless + Kalita 155, 15g/225g (1:15), EG-1 6.4, 92C, straight MgCl2 (GH ~44 / KH 0). Front phase owns sweetness depth: 45g bloom (Dial 0, 25s) -> P1 to 110g then CLOSED 20s steep. Back half owns brightness: P2 to 170g on Dial 6, P3 to 225g on Dial 7, ~4:00 total.
- Three-brew arc findings: the closed steep is the sweetness-depth lever; the walnut/peanut-tea register is CONTACT-derived (restricted back half), not roast-derived - thin it by opening the valve, not grinding coarser; mandarin needs a bright short back half (D5 buries it). Temperature flip: the optimized recipe reads best HOT-WARM, opposite the xBloom gate cup which needed cooling - the cooling-curve read is vehicle-dependent on this lot.

## Carry-forward (for the next fixed-FC natural / the Finca Deborah V1)

- **Primary lever:** bean-temp drop ceiling against the fixed ~205C FC. Post-FC climb ~1C/30s makes it nonlinear - bracket in 1C steps, not 2C (the 2C step made V3 bimodal).
- **Peak inlet moves FC time only;** at 242C peak the energy budget cannot reach FC at all. Gentler early ramp moves nothing.
- **Development is color-driven, not dev-time-driven** on this variety - do not chase dev-time targets; 222's 80s integrated mandarin BETTER than 221's 61s even as both disqualified.
- **Finca Deborah starting hypothesis:** find the lot's architectural FC temp with one probe, anchor a bean-temp auto-drop AT or 1C above it for an FC-onset slot, bracket upward 1C at a time, evaluate from the light end. Do not chase florals via development. High-elevation density/FC-floor assumptions do NOT transfer uncritically from this 1,130m lot.
- **Cup-vs-structure inversion in a V-set** = suspect the whole set is in the wrong response-surface region (all-past-FC artifact), not a tuning signal.
