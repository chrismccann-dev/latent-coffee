# Pink Bourbon — per-cultivar brewing patterns

*Coffee Research · Latent · Brewing Historian cluster · by-cultivar*

**Corpus:** N=5 brews across three processes (clean anaerobic washed, anoxic anaerobic natural, dark-room-dried natural) + the Rosado sibling (n=1, kept as a [Sub-Threshold Cultivar Candidate](docs/skills/brewing-historian/cluster/patterns/cross-coffee-insights.md#sub-threshold-cultivar-candidates)). Graduated from the CCIL sub-threshold list 2026-10-02 ([pruning case 016](docs/sprints/pruning-cases/016-brewing-historian-hybrid-consolidate.md)).

**Variety default:** transparency-driven, not weight-driven. **Balanced Intensity is the ceiling; Full Expression does not pay** - the variety reads cleanest at moderate extraction (Colombian washed / anaerobic-washed Pink Bourbon, [by-strategy/balanced-intensity.md](docs/skills/brewing-historian/cluster/patterns/by-strategy/balanced-intensity.md)). Clean anaerobic *washed* sits clarity-side, not Suppression.

**Process overrides the variety ceiling - twice (the DRD natural on two roasts of the same lot):**

- **Anoxic / sealed-container natural → full phase separation.** Component Coffee Colombia Terra Pink Bourbon Anaerobic Natural (Huila community lot, 14hr anoxic, WB 80.8 light; brew `dc886312`, 2026-06-05) blew through the Balanced ceiling and resolved only on **Hybrid (Temperature-Staged)** - hot front for reach, cool back for a clean tail. This extends the Rosado lesson (Scenery Pikudo's anoxic natural needing Full Expression) one step further. Rule: do not apply Pink Bourbon ceiling logic to anoxic / heavy-anaerobic natural lots; expect the process to dominate.
- **Dark-room-dried natural at WB 70.1 → ICS recovery shape, no roast wall.** Untold Coffee Lab Brazil Fazenda Um Pink Bourbon DRD Natural (TUVADKNAT; brew `91a014fd`, 2026-07-27) is the boundary marker that the developed-roast wall is ABSENT at 70.1 ([by-roast-level/medium-developed.md](docs/skills/brewing-historian/cluster/patterns/by-roast-level/medium-developed.md)); the Intensity-Clarity Split did buried-note recovery (hold the medium paper, tiny cut, 1:45 steep), not tail draining.
  - **Same farm and lot, Latent-roasted (BRA-FAZENDAUM-PB-DRD-2026, Batch 233; brew `e3ecb455`, 2026-09-07) → Hybrid (Sequential) on the Switch.** Planned Balanced Intensity and re-zoned, so the DRD natural has now left the variety ceiling on both roasts. What the Latent roast changed: the cup moved from Untold's raspberry tea + residual smoke to jasmine / honey / raspberry / jasmine green tea with a layered-evolving arc (raspberry is the quiet layer; a woody/green-tea earthiness stays part of the cup). In the side-by-side on the plain-percolation recipe the frozen Untold dose read nutty/roasty with sour-bitter edges and Batch 233 won on back-cleanliness and sweetness/acidity. Recipe shape inverted with it: CONE FAST and no cut (a short, clean back instead of the medium paper + tiny cut), finer (6.0 vs 6.6) and cooler (90°C vs 92°C). Shared across both roasts: an immersion front at 94-95°C over-extracts this coffee - the temperature rule is in [by-strategy/hybrid.md](docs/skills/brewing-historian/cluster/patterns/by-strategy/hybrid.md) § Sequential. Operator calibration: evolving but short of the PicoLot bar. Untested headroom: coarser grind, Apax sweetness-side dosing.

**Clean anaerobic washed, light → Clarity-First upper edge, no iteration needed.** Moonwake Ilde Burbano El Pilón (Palestina, Huila, 1740m; 24h anaerobic, double-washed; brew `8cc88312`, 2026-06-17): EG-1 6.5 / 94°C / Kalita Wave 155 + xBloom Premium / 1:16 delivered on Brew 1. Layered-evolving cup - grape-over-green-tea reads separated hot/warm and integrates to green grape + blackberry + bergamot at the ~45°C cool peak. Evaluate at the cool peak (Cooling Arc is a diagnostic, not a recipe lever).
