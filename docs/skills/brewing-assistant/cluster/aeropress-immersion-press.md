The immersion-press form of the **Concentrated Pour-Over** style (CONTEXT-brewing headword): the gentlest contact mechanism in the RP8 mechanism map — the boundary-breaker (coherent below the 1:9 percolation boundary) and the astringency-free form (zero verdict-stage astringency across 5 cups on a pressure-punishing coffee). ## Method (amended lock)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Brewer | AeroPress Premium, **inverted**, paper micro-filter (flow-control cap + gold-tone excluded) |
| Ratio | **1:10** (15 g / 150 g) — the style optimum under the press mechanism too |
| Grind | EG-1 @ 7.0 |
| Water | 89°C (RP8 reference: plain PA tap, zero additions) |
| Pour | One continuous pour |
| Agitation | 2 stirs only (one N-S, one E-W, saturation only — no heavy agitation) |
| Steep | To 1:00 |
| Flip | ~10 s |
| Press | Very slow, to first sustained hiss — **≈50-55 s** |
| Total | **~2:00-2:05** |
| Yield | ~115-124 g (bed retention ~30-41 g) |
| TDS | 1.13-1.20% at 1:10 |

## Serving forms (co-equal)

1. **Direct:** 1:10 undiluted — round, layered, punchier.
2. **Bypass-to-optimum:** 1:9 concentrate (15 g / 135 g) + ~20% bypass water to ~1:10-strength — trades a little punch for openness/sparkle/brightness; operator preference in direct comparison (RP8 T5 buffer cup, against-known-identity pick).

The style's identity is **"no dilution past optimum"**, not "no bypass": bypass is a strength-targeting tool from a stronger concentrate (bypass-to-optimum won; bypass-past-optimum read thin and lost).

## Operational notes

- **Concentration boundary is mechanism-dependent:** immersion-press stays coherent at 1:7.5 where percolation ladders collapsed at 1:9. Optimum strength (~1:10 / ~1.1% TDS) is mechanism-independent; the boundary and failure modes are what change.
- **Repeatability:** press-to-hiss leaves ±~9 g yield slack → ±~0.07% TDS. Profile reproduces cross-day; press to a target yield if tighter TDS repeats matter.
- **Mechanism choice is a character choice, not a quality ranking:** valve-driven percolation at 1:10 gives percolation clarity character; immersion-press at 1:10-strength gives the round, layered, astringency-free form. Serving-side drops layer on either.
